PHASE 3: PERIOD MEDIANS (GEE EXPORT)
====================================

Status: COMPLETE. Eight files exported from Google Earth Engine and downloaded.
Resampling to the DEM grid is in Phase 3b.


1. PURPOSE
----------
The model trains on 2002 to 2015 and tests on 2016 to 2024. Four predictors
change with time (NDVI, NDMI, LST and rainfall), so each needs one layer per
period. The earlier full-period layers (2002 to 2024) cannot be used for the
split, since they contain the test years in their medians. This phase exports
the period-specific layers. The DEM, slope, aspect, distance layers and LULC
are fixed in time and are not exported again.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
One GEE script (period_medians_all_eight.js) queues eight exports to the
Google Drive folder Viphya_Data. Each export is clipped to the Chikangawa
boundary and written in EPSG:32736 at its native resolution. The files were
then downloaded to this phase's outputs folder.

Study boundary used in GEE: projects/rainfallpatterns/assets/Chikangawa_forest


3. INPUT COLLECTIONS (GEE)
--------------------------
  NDVI:      MODIS/061/MOD13A3, band NDVI (scale 0.0001)
  NDMI:      MODIS/061/MOD09A1, bands sur_refl_b02 (NIR) and sur_refl_b06
             (SWIR), scale 0.0001, clear-sky mask from StateQA bits 0 and 1
  LST:       MODIS/061/MOD11A2, band LST_Day_1km (scale 0.02, minus 273.15)
  Rainfall:  UCSB-CHG/CHIRPS/DAILY, band precipitation

Dates and filters: July to December only, 1 July to 31 December in each year.
Period 1: 2002-01-01 to 2015-12-31. Period 2: 2016-01-01 to 2024-12-31.


4. PROCESSING PER LAYER
-----------------------
  NDVI:      median of the July to December composites
  NDMI:      NDMI per clear-sky image, then median
  LST:       zero values masked (no data), then median, then converted to Celsius
  Rainfall:  mean daily rainfall across the period, times 184 days

Notes on these numbers:
- The valid share differs by layer. The vegetation layers (55.19 percent)
  and LST (56.91 percent) use different grids. Rainfall (64.17 percent)
  uses 5.5 km cells, so it covers the boundary with fewer, larger cells.
  Phase 3b must handle this, and the dissertation must state it.
- The test period is drier and warmer than the training period on every
  layer: NDMI mean falls from 0.096 to 0.077, LST rises from 26.3 to 27.1 C,
  and rainfall falls from 343 to 310 mm. The model is tested on conditions
  it did not see in training. Say so in the discussion, since it explains
  some of any drop in test performance.
- The NDVI and NDMI values match the earlier full-period ranges, which is a
  sanity check that the exports are correct.


6. WHY THESE CHOICES
--------------------
- Period split: the test years must not appear in any training predictor.
  Splitting the fire labels alone would leave the 2016 to 2024 years inside
  the training predictors.
- LST zeros: MOD11A2 uses zero for no data. Scaling zero gives -273 C and
  pulls the median down. The earlier LST layers may have this error, so they
  are superseded.
- Native resolution: the GEE export keeps the source resolution. Resampling
  to the 30.63 m DEM grid is done in Python (Phase 3b), so every layer is
  resampled the same way and the method can be recorded in one place.
- Rainfall as a mean: a seasonal total is a sum over days, so the mean of
  daily values times 184 is the correct statistic. Medians are used for the
  other three layers, so the statistic differs between layers.


7. WHAT THE DISSERTATION SHOULD NOTE
------------------------------------
- Period-specific predictors: NDVI, NDMI, LST and rainfall are medians or
  means over 2002 to 2015 (training) and 2016 to 2024 (testing).
- Source resolutions: NDVI and NDMI 500 m (MODIS), LST 1 km (MODIS MOD11A2,
  daytime only), rainfall about 5.5 km (CHIRPS, mean seasonal total).
- LST is daytime only. Night LST was not used.
- Rainfall statistic: mean seasonal total, which differs from the medians used
  for the vegetation and LST layers.
- Superseded layers: the earlier full-period LST and rainfall files
  (viphya_lst_30m, viphya_rainfall) are not used in the model. Say so in the
  methods to explain the change from the proposal.
