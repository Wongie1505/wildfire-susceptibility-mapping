PHASE 9: 2025 OVERLAY (FIRE DETECTIONS ON THE SUSCEPTIBILITY MAP)
=================================================================

Status: PARTIAL. The map is produced and the detection total is confirmed at 234.
The class counts for detections and matched non-fire points are still to be run.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Check the 2016 to 2024 susceptibility map against fires from a year outside the
training and map periods (2025). Count how many 2025 fire detections fall in each
class, then do the same for matched non-fire points. The unit is the detection
(decision D30).


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
1. Downloaded the MODIS fire archive for 2025 from FIRMS (Collection 6.1, Malawi,
   Shapefile). File: fire_archive_M-C61_847303.
2. Kept confidence 75 and above.
3. Clipped to the ROI (Chikangawa boundary, D16). Result: 234 detections.
4. Drew the map with the 2025 detections as a density surface over the 2016 to
   2024 class map. Script: map_2025_overlay.py.

Still to do:
5. Count the 234 detections by class (low, medium, high, not predicted).
6. Draw matched non-fire points: 1:1 with detections, 500 m buffer from 2025
   detections, inside the ROI, seed 42. Script: non-fire sampling script
   (shapefile output).
7. Count the non-fire points by class.
8. Compare the shares: fire share over non-fire share in each class, with the
   lift and a 95 percent interval.


3. INPUT FILES
--------------
  rf_class_2016_2024.tif                 class map (Phase 7b deliverables)
  fire_archive_M-C61_847303 (shapefile)  2025 MODIS detections, confidence 75
                                         and above, clipped to the ROI
  Chikangawa_forest.shp                  study boundary (ROI)


4. OUTPUT FILES SO FAR
----------------------
  Map_2025_Overlay.png     susceptibility map with the 2025 detection hotspots


5. RESULTS SO FAR
-----------------
  2025 detections used: 234 (confidence 75 and above, inside the ROI).
  Hotspot areas drawn: 6, from a density threshold of 0.6 on a surface scaled
  from 0 to 1, with a kernel bandwidth of 0.1.

  Counts by class: NOT YET RUN.

  Earlier event-based check (sensitivity only, not the main result):
  An earlier run used 157 events (156 classified, 1 not predicted) and an equal
  number of matched non-fire points. The shares were:
    Low:    7.69 percent of events, 9.55 percent of non-fire points, lift 0.81
    Medium: 52.56 percent of events, 50.96 percent of non-fire points, lift 1.03
    High:   39.74 percent of events, 39.49 percent of non-fire points, lift 1.01
  A lift near 1 means the map does no better than a random point in that class.
  This run used events, and D30 makes detections the main unit, so these figures
  are reported only as a sensitivity check. They must be rerun on detections.


6. OPEN CHECKS BEFORE THE DISSERTATION USES THIS
------------------------------------------------
(a) Window for the 234. The methods must state the date window used to produce
    234. The 2002 to 2024 analysis uses July to December. If the file includes
    January to June 2025, state that and justify it, or refilter to July to
    December and report that count alongside 234. The count of 234 is confirmed,
    but its window must be written down.

(b) Layout defect in Map_2025_Overlay.png. The title and subtitle run past the
    right edge of the figure. Shorten the text or reduce the font size before the
    figure is used.

(c) Straight edges in the class map. The map shows a straight vertical edge on
    the western side of the ROI and a rectangular block with straight sides in
    the south-east. These are likely the edges of a predictor's valid area (the
    rainfall layer is about 5.5 km; the MODIS layers cover a rectangular extent),
    not a model fault. The 2 percent of the ROI with no prediction (Phase 7) sits
    at these edges. Check in QGIS which predictor causes each edge and state the
    cause in the methods or the map caption.


7. HOTSPOT THRESHOLD
--------------------
The hatched areas use a density threshold of 0.6 on a surface scaled from 0 to 1,
with a kernel bandwidth of 0.1. The threshold is a choice, not a result. The
dissertation must state both values. Changing the threshold changes which areas
are hatched.


8. WHAT THE DISSERTATION SHOULD NOTE
------------------------------------
- The 2025 overlay checks the map on fires outside the training and map periods
  (D29, D30).
- The unit is the detection. Confidence 75 and above. Clipped to the ROI.
- The density surface is a visual aid. It is not a fire forecast and is not a
  measure of accuracy.
- The points are located to about 1 km, so a point near a class boundary can
  fall in either class.
- The hotspot threshold and bandwidth are stated (section 7).
