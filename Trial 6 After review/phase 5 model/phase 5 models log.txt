PHASE 5: MODELS (CROSS-VALIDATION ON THE TRAINING YEARS)
========================================================

Status: COMPLETE. Three models trained and cross-validated on 2002 to 2015 only.
The test years were not used. The main model (Random Forest) is declared in
decision D17 before any test scoring.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Train the three models on the training years, and measure how well each one
generalises, using cross-validation inside those years. Save the fitted models
for Phase 6, which scores them once on the test years.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
1. Loaded training_table.csv and kept the 2002 to 2015 rows only (4,860 rows,
   2,430 fire and 2,430 non-fire).
2. Ran two cross-validation schemes inside the training years, 5 folds each:
   - spatial block: whole 5 km blocks held out (GroupKFold on block_id)
   - random: stratified random folds (for comparison only)
3. Fitted the three models on all training rows and saved them.
4. Wrote the cross-validation results, the summary, and the settings used.

Folder: Phase_5_models/
Script: phase5_models.py


3. INPUT FILES
--------------
Folder data/table/:
  training_table.csv   (from Phase 4 outputs)


4. OUTPUT FILES
---------------
Folder outputs/:
  cross_validation.csv    one row per model, scheme and fold
  model_performance.csv   mean and standard deviation per model and scheme
  model_settings.csv      every setting used by each model

Folder outputs/models/:
  RandomForest.joblib
  XGBoost.joblib
  LightGBM.joblib


5. SETTINGS
-----------
  Random Forest: 300 trees, max depth 20, balanced class weights, seed 42
  XGBoost:       300 trees, max depth 6, learning rate 0.05, subsample 0.8,
                 column sample 0.8, seed 42
  LightGBM:      300 trees, max depth 6, learning rate 0.05, subsample 0.8,
                 column sample 0.8, seed 42, default leaves
  Threshold for the classification scores: 0.5
  No grid search was run. These are fixed values.


6. RESULTS (cross-validation, training years)
---------------------------------------------
  Model          Scheme          AUC           F1            Precision     Recall
  RandomForest   random          0.925 +/- 0.009   0.844   0.849   0.839
  XGBoost        random          0.907 +/- 0.010   0.819   0.829   0.811
  LightGBM       random          0.902 +/- 0.009   0.814   0.820   0.809
  RandomForest   spatial block   0.773 +/- 0.033   0.709   0.646   0.791
  XGBoost        spatial block   0.759 +/- 0.038   0.692   0.639   0.758
  LightGBM       spatial block   0.761 +/- 0.038   0.695   0.642   0.762

The random and spatial schemes differ by about 0.15 to 0.17 AUC for every
model.


7. WHY IT MATTERS
-----------------
- The spatial result is the honest estimate. Random folds let neighbouring
  fires sit on both sides of a split, which inflates the score. The 0.93 AUC
  from the earlier analysis came from random folds.
- The three models are not clearly different on the spatial scheme. Random
  Forest is highest, but its margin over the others (about 0.015 AUC) is
  smaller than one standard deviation. This is why the main model was declared
  in D17 on the training years, not chosen by the test scores.
- Recall is higher than precision on the spatial scheme (about 0.79 against
  0.65). In unseen blocks the models flag more area as fire than the fires
  that occur. Expect the same pattern in the map.
