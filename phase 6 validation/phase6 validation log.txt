Status: COMPLETE. The test years were scored once. The results are recorded in

1. PURPOSE
----------
Score the saved models on the test years, which were not used in training, and
measure whether the model generalises to later years. Also measure which
predictors matter on the test rows, and how the result depends on the
non-fire buffer.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
1. Scored the three saved models once on the 3,540 test rows (1,770 fire,
   1,770 non-fire), with a 95 percent interval from resampling the test rows
   (1,000 resamples).
2. Computed permutation importance for the Random Forest on the test rows
   (10 repeats per predictor, AUC as the score).
3. Retrained the Random Forest at 0, 250 and 1000 m, with the same settings, and
   scored each on its own test rows. The 500 m result comes from the main table.

Folder: Phase_6_validation/
Script: phase6_validation.py

6. WHY IT MATTERS
-----------------
- The test AUC is 0.57. That is above chance at 500 m, but the skill is weak.
  The spatial cross-validation AUC of 0.77 and the random-fold AUC of 0.93 do
  not describe performance in later years.
- The buffer result shows the artifact described in D10. AUC rises with buffer
  size, from 0.52 at 0 m to 0.67 at 1000 m. A larger buffer keeps the
  non-fire points far from fires, which makes them easier to separate. The
  1000 m score is inflated by that, not by better prediction.
- The predictors that matter most are static: the DEM, settlement distance and
  roads. The time-varying layers contribute little. LST has almost no
  importance. The model identifies fire-prone terrain and proximity to
  settlements. It does not track the year-to-year climate signal.
- Precision (0.53) is below recall (0.70). The model flags more area than the
  fires that occur, as it did in the spatial cross-validation.


7. HOW TO READ THE 0 AND 250 M RESULTS
--------------------------------------
Near-chance AUC at 0 and 250 m can mean either of two things:
(a) the model has little real skill, or (b) the non-fire points include
unrecorded fires, which pulls AUC toward 0.5. The data cannot separate them.
The dissertation must state both.
