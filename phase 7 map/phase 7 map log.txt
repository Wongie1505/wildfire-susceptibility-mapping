PHASE 7: MAP AND AREAS
======================

Status: SCRIPT WRITTEN. Not yet run. The log is completed after the run.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Apply the Random Forest to the full study area, using the 2016 to 2024
predictors, to produce a susceptibility map. Classify it, and measure the area
in each class. The map is a relative susceptibility surface (decision D9, D21).
It is not a probability of fire in a given year.


2. WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES
-----------------------
1. Loads the 12 predictors in the same order used to train the model in Phase 5.
2. Keeps only cells inside the ROI (Chikangawa boundary, D16) with all 12
   predictors valid.
3. Predicts a susceptibility score for each of those cells, in chunks of
   500,000.
4. Classifies the score: low below 0.33, medium 0.33 to below 0.66, high 0.66
   and above (D23).
5. Writes the score and the class as GeoTIFFs on the DEM grid.
6. Calculates the area of each class in hectares and square kilometres.
7. Copies the four period predictors (2016 to 2024) to outputs/predictors/.

Folder: Phase_7_map/
Script: phase7_map.py

Prediction summary (from prediction_summary.csv):
  Cell size 30.63 m, cell area 938.45 m2
  Cells not predicted inside the ROI: 32,917 (2.0 percent), where at least one
  predictor is missing, mostly at the edges

Note on the mean score: the training sample was balanced (50 percent fire), so
a neutral predictor set would give a mean near 0.5. The mean of 0.61 reflects
the 2016 to 2024 conditions pushing scores up across the landscape. See D27.


6. WHY IT MATTERS
-----------------
- Area per class is the number a forest manager will use. It is only as good
  as the model. With a test AUC of 0.57 (D19), the map should be presented as
  a relative ranking of areas, not as a precise fire forecast.
- The class limits and the grid are fixed in the script, so the figures can
  be reproduced exactly.


7. WHAT THE DISSERTATION SHOULD NOTE
------------------------------------
- Map source: the Random Forest trained on 2002 to 2015 (D17), applied to the
  2016 to 2024 predictors (D9).
- Susceptibility score, not probability (D21).
- Class limits: 0.33 and 0.66 (D23).
- Cell size 30.63 m, cell area 938.45 m2. State this in the area table caption.
- Study area: the Chikangawa ROI, 1,577.3 km2 (D16). Areas are reported as a
  share of the predicted area and of the ROI.
- The map and class figures in the author's earlier image set are from the old
  model and must not be used (D24). Use only figures from these outputs.
- Map titles and legend labels follow D25.
