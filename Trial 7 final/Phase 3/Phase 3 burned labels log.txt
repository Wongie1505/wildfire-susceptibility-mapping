PHASE 3: BURNED-AREA LABELS (TRIAL 7)
=====================================

Status: COMPLETE. Date run: 15 November 2026.

Context. Trial 7 is a declared new analysis, started after the Trial 6 test
results were known (Random Forest test AUC 0.571). No model has been trained
in Trial 7 and no test result was used for any choice in this phase.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Build the response variable for every 500 m forest cell and every fire season
from 2002 to 2024: did the cell burn between 1 July and 31 December?

Trial 6 used FIRMS active-fire points as the "fire" class and drew random
points as the "no fire" class, kept 500 m away from every detection. That
design had two weaknesses:
  (a) A FIRMS point is a snapshot of a hot spot. Fires between satellite
      passes, under cloud or smoke, or too small to detect were never
      recorded, so some "no fire" points had actually burned.
  (b) The 500 m buffer made the "no fire" points systematically far from
      fires. Part of the model's skill was distance to a recorded fire, which
      is why the buffer sensitivity test showed AUC rising with the buffer.

Trial 7 replaces both. Burned area maps the burn scar, which stays visible for
weeks, and every forest cell is labelled in every season. No point is invented.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
- Exported, from Google Earth Engine, two files per fire season (46 files):
    burned_YYYY.tif     1 = burned, 0 = not burned, -9999 = no usable label
    burn_day_YYYY.tif   day of year of the first burn that season, 0 = not
                        burned, -9999 = no usable label
- Checked that all 46 files sit exactly on the Phase 2 grid.
- Blanked every cell outside the forest, using the mask from static_stack.tif.
- Counted burned and not-burned cells per season.
- Compared the labels with the old Trial 6 FIRMS fire events.

No multi-band stacks were made in this phase. Each season is its own file.


3. HOW IT WAS DONE
------------------
Scripts: gee_burned_area.js (Earth Engine), build_labels.py (Spyder).

Source: MODIS MCD64A1 Collection 6.1 monthly burned area, asset
MODIS/061/MCD64A1, bands BurnDate and QA. Native resolution about 463 m
(sinusoidal grid), nominally 500 m.

Step 1. Fire season. Six monthly images per year, July to December. The
  script prints the number of images per season, and it should be 6 for every
  year.
Step 2. Burned label. A cell is burned (1) if BurnDate is above 0 in any of
  the six months, otherwise 0. GEE can mask BurnDate = 0, so masked values are
  filled with 0 before the test (see section 8).
Step 3. Burn day. The earliest BurnDate in the season (day of year). For
  reference, 182 is 1 July, 244 is 1 September, 274 is 1 October, 305 is
  1 November and 335 is 1 December in a non-leap year.
Step 4. Quality rule. The script can require good QA (land and sufficient
  valid data, QA bits 0 and 1) in a minimum number of months before accepting
  a "not burned" label. MIN_VALID_MONTHS was set to 0, so no QA requirement
  was applied. A burned cell is always kept.
Step 5. Grid. Exported with crs EPSG:32736 and an explicit crsTransform, so
  the cells line up exactly with static_stack.tif (500 m, origin snapped to
  multiples of 500). The 463 m source is resampled to the 500 m grid by
  nearest neighbour, the default.
Step 6. Cleaning in Python. For each year the script checks the grid against
  static_stack.tif and stops with a message if it differs, sets cells outside
  the forest to -9999, and writes clean single-band files.
Step 7. FIRMS check. The Trial 6 events (fire_events.csv, July to December,
  confidence filter and clustering as defined in Trial 6) were converted to
  EPSG:32736 and assigned to a 500 m cell. For each event the script asks
  whether that cell, and whether any of the 3 x 3 cells around it, is burned
  in the same year.


4. WHY EACH CHOICE WAS MADE
---------------------------
- Burned area instead of active-fire points: it records that a fire happened,
  not just that something was hot during a satellite pass.
- Every cell labelled every season: removes the invented "no fire" points and
  the buffer artifact described in section 1.
- One row per cell per season (built in Phase 5): the model can use that
  season's rainfall, dryness and fuel, which Trial 6 could not.
- July to December: carried over from Trial 6. It still needs checking against
  the burn day histogram (section 12).
- MIN_VALID_MONTHS = 0: the strict first version removed nearly every
  "not burned" cell (section 8). QA only matters in cloudy months, and July to
  December is mostly dry season in Malawi. Raise it only after checking the
  "Mean valid months" console output.
- Nearest neighbour resampling: burned or not is a category. Averaging would
  create fractions that are not labels.
- Labels from MCD64A1 only, as the main result: one published, peer-reviewed
  source with documented accuracy. A union with FIRMS is kept for a declared
  sensitivity test (section 11).
- Study period ends in 2024: set in Trial 6 and kept. 2025 was considered as
  an untouched test year and not used. The 2016 to 2024 test years were already
  scored in Trial 6, so they are not blind in the strict sense. This must be
  stated in the limitations.

7. RESULTS
----------
Labels (label_counts.csv):
  Cells in the forest: 6,306 per season. Cells with no label: 0 in every
  season. Labelled cell-seasons: 145,038 (6,306 x 23).
  Burned: 21,517 (14.8%). Not burned: 123,521 (85.2%).
  Burned share by season ranges from 3.6% (2006) to 30.2% (2016).
  Mean of the 23 seasons: 14.8%.
  The model therefore trains on imbalanced classes, about 1 burned to 5.7 not
  burned. Phase 6 will handle this with class weights, not by deleting cells.

FIRMS agreement (firms_agreement.csv, TOTAL row):
  FIRMS events: 4,244. On labelled cells: 4,228 (16 fall on cells outside
  the forest mask).
  Event falls in a burned cell:     1,194 of 4,228 = 28.2%
  Event within 1 cell of a burn:    2,419 of 4,228 = 57.2%
  Share of all cells that burned:   14.8% (the agreement expected by chance
  for the same-cell test)
  Same-cell agreement is about 1.9 times the chance level overall, and above
  chance in all 23 seasons (ratio from 1.4 in 2016 to 2.9 in 2007).
  Agreement rises in heavy fire years (2015 and 2016 are the highest, at 39.8%
  and 41.9%), which is what real signal would do.

Reading the result: the labels carry real fire information, but they are not
perfect. About 72% of FIRMS events sit in a cell that MCD64A1 maps as not
burned.


8. CHALLENGES AND FIXES
-----------------------
1. First GEE version produced only burned cells. The first run gave a burned
   share of 100% and 21,517 rows instead of about 145,000, and FIRMS agreement
   of 100%. Every "not burned" cell had lost its label. The script required
   good QA in all six months, and treated a masked BurnDate as missing data.
   The most likely cause is that GEE masks BurnDate = 0, so unburned cells
   looked like missing cells. This was not proved separately. Fix: masked
   BurnDate is now filled with 0 before testing, and the QA rule is switched
   off with MIN_VALID_MONTHS = 0. The rerun gave 145,038 labelled cell-seasons.
   Note that 21,517 is exactly the burned count of the corrected run, which
   confirms that only burned cells survived in the first version.
2. Confusion over stacks. Exporting 23 bands in one file made the outputs hard
   to read. Changed to one file per season and per variable, and the Phase 3
   output is individual files only.
3. Choice of end year. 2025 was considered. It was not used, because the
   predictors and Phase 2 to 4 work are built to 2024.


9. LIMITATIONS
--------------
- MCD64A1 can miss small burns and burns under canopy. Chikangawa is a pine
  plantation, and the FIRMS check suggests a real share of fires is missed.
  This is a likely explanation and not a proven one.
- Cells labelled "not burned" therefore include some that burned. Label noise
  is reduced against Trial 6, not removed.
- Source pixels are about 463 m and the grid is 500 m. Nearest neighbour
  sampling skips about 14% of source pixels (1 minus (463.3 / 500) squared)
  and can shift a burn by up to half a pixel.
- A FIRMS point comes from a roughly 1 km pixel with geolocation error, so
  perfect same-cell agreement is not expected. The 3 x 3 result is the fairer
  comparison.
- With MIN_VALID_MONTHS = 0, a cell with poor observations in several months
  can be labelled not burned.
- The 2016 to 2024 years were scored in Trial 6, so they are not a blind test.

  Collection 6.1 monthly burned-area product. A cell was labelled burned if
  any monthly image between 1 July and 31 December recorded a burn date, and
  every forest cell was labelled in every season from 2002 to 2024. No
  non-fire points were sampled, so no buffer around fires was needed."
- State why this replaced FIRMS points plus random non-fire points (section 1).
- Report the label summary (section 7) and the FIRMS agreement: 28.2% in the

- State the class balance (14.8% burned) and that class weights, not

11. DECISIONS TO ADD TO THE PROJECT DECISIONS LOG
-------------------------------------------------
T7-6.  Response variable: MCD64A1 C6.1 burned area, one label per 500 m cell
       per fire season, July to December, 2002 to 2024. No random non-fire
       points and no buffer.
       Reason: removes label noise from missed fires and the buffer artifact.
T7-7.  QA rule off (MIN_VALID_MONTHS = 0) for "not burned" cells.
       Reason: the strict rule removed almost all unburned cells.
T7-8.  Nearest neighbour resampling from the 463 m source to the 500 m grid.
T7-9.  MCD64A1-only labels are the main result. A union label (MCD64A1 burned
       OR a FIRMS event inside the cell) is a sensitivity test, declared here
       before it is run, and reported whatever it shows. It is judged on
       training spatial CV only, never on the test years.
T7-10. Study period ends in 2024. 2025 not used.
T7-11. Outputs are individual files per season. Stacks are not used, apart
       from static_stack.tif.
