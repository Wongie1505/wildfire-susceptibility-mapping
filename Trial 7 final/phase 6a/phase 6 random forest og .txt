PHASE 6: RANDOM FOREST, THREE-RUN CHECK (TRIAL 7)
=================================================

Status: COMPLETE. Date run: 15 November 2026.
Runs: 6a (both fire history predictors), 6b (burn count only), 6c (years since
burn only). Selected for Phase 7: 6a.

Context. Trial 7 is a declared new analysis, started after the Trial 6 test
results were known (Random Forest test AUC 0.571). In this phase the test
years (2016 to 2024) were not loaded or used in any way.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Train and tune the Random Forest on the training years, check how well it
generalises in space and in time, and settle one open question from Phase 5:
two fire history predictors (burn_count_3yr and years_since_burn) are almost
the same information (Spearman rho -0.945). Does the model do better with both,
or with one of them? The question is answered by three separate, separately
tuned runs.

  6a   both predictors            14 predictors
  6b   burn_count_3yr only        13 predictors
  6c   years_since_burn only      13 predictors

Each run has its own folder and its own copy of modelling_table.csv.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
For each run, on the training rows only (88,284 rows, 2002 to 2015):
- Tried 12 Random Forest settings and scored each by spatial-block
  cross-validation.
- Took the best setting and scored it again with spatial-block cross-validation
  (300 trees) and with forward chaining (train on earlier years, score the
  next year).
- Fitted a final model on all training rows and saved it.
- Compared the three runs under a rule that was declared before the runs.


3. HOW IT WAS DONE
------------------
Scripts: phase6a_both.py, phase6b_burn_count.py, phase6c_years_since.py. They
are identical except for the two lines that set PHASE and VARIANT.

Model: scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier with class_weight
"balanced_subsample", because only about 14% of rows are burned. Random seed
42. Test rows were removed from memory straight after the table was loaded.

Step 1. Settings search. 12 settings were drawn at random (seed 42) from 54
  combinations:
    max_depth          6, 10, 14
    min_samples_leaf   10, 30, 80
    max_features       sqrt, 0.3, 0.5
    max_samples        0.3, 0.5  (share of rows each tree sees)
  Each setting used 100 trees and was scored by 5-fold spatial-block
  cross-validation: GroupKFold on the 5 km block_id, so whole blocks are held
  out. The score is the mean AUC over the 5 folds. The highest mean wins.
Step 2. Spatial-block cross-validation of the winner, with 300 trees, 5 folds.
Step 3. Forward chaining of the winner, with 300 trees. For each year t from
  2009 to 2015, train on all years before t and score year t. The first step
  trains on 2002 to 2008 (7 years). The validation is not blocked in space: the
  same cells appear in training and validation, in different years. This copies
  the real test, which also scores the same area in later years.
Step 4. Final model: all 88,284 training rows, 300 trees, saved as
  rf_model.joblib. Impurity importance is saved only as a sanity check. The
  reported importance will be grouped permutation importance in Phase 7.
Step 5. Compute control: the script uses half of the available cores (2 cores
  on the author's computer), saves after every setting and can be stopped and
  resumed.

The rule for choosing between runs (declared in the Phase 5 log as T7-24, with
the tie rule stated in conversation before any result was seen):
  Choose the run with the highest mean spatial-block CV AUC. If the best two
  differ by less than 0.005, keep 6a (both predictors). Forward chaining is
  reported but not used to choose.


4. WHY EACH CHOICE WAS MADE
---------------------------
- Three separate runs, each tuned: a fair comparison needs each variant at its
  best settings. Comparing at one shared setting could favour one variant by
  chance.
- Spatial-block CV as the selection score: it is the headline validation in
  Trial 6 (D18), it was named in T7-24, and it removes the advantage a model
  gets from seeing a neighbouring cell.
- Forward chaining as a reported diagnostic: the Trial 6 failure was in time
  (spatial CV 0.773, test 0.571), so a temporal check on training years is the
  nearest thing to the test that does not touch the test years.
- Class weights, not resampling: no rows are deleted or invented, and the
  burned share stays at its real level.
- Random search of 12 settings: a full grid of 54 would take four times
  longer on a laptop, and the differences between nearby settings are small.
- Settings chosen on training data only, so the test years cannot influence the
  model.

7. RESULTS
----------
Winning setting. All three runs chose the same one:
  max_depth 14, min_samples_leaf 30, max_features 0.5, max_samples 0.5
  (setting number 8 of 12 in every run).

Range of mean AUC across the 12 settings:
  6a  0.720 to 0.758      6b  0.712 to 0.745      6c  0.719 to 0.756

Comparison of the three runs (training years only):

  Run  Predictors         Spatial CV AUC (sd)   Forward chaining AUC
  6a   both               0.759 (0.0138)        0.6615
  6b   burn count only    0.746 (0.0169)        0.6327
  6c   years since only   0.758 (0.0145)        0.6589

Forward chaining by year (burned share of the validation year in brackets):

  Year (burned %)   6a      6b      6c
  2009 (8.4)        0.604   0.580   0.601
  2010 (22.0)       0.590   0.581   0.591
  2011 (10.6)       0.696   0.681   0.697
  2012 (24.0)       0.641   0.628   0.646
  2013 (18.8)       0.684   0.650   0.682
  2014 (11.6)       0.736   0.693   0.729
  2015 (28.0)       0.681   0.617   0.667

Average of the last five years (2011 to 2015): 6a 0.687, 6b 0.654, 6c 0.684.
Average of the first two (2009 and 2010):      6a 0.597, 6b 0.580, 6c 0.596.

Outcome of the rule. 6a and 6c differ by 0.0015 in spatial CV AUC, which is
less than 0.005, so 6a (both predictors) is kept. 6b is 0.013 below 6a in
spatial CV and 0.029 below it in forward chaining, so burn count alone is
clearly the weakest. Forward chaining, which was not used to choose, gives the
same order: 6a, then 6c, then 6b.

Reading the result:
- Years since burn carries most of the fire history signal. Adding burn count
  to it gives a very small gain, inside the noise.
- Spatial CV (0.759) is close to the Trial 6 spatial CV (0.773 plus or minus
  0.033). The model finds fire-prone ground it has not seen about as well as
  before.
- Forward chaining averages 0.66 for 6a. The Trial 6 test AUC was 0.571. This
  is evidence that the Trial 7 design gives more temporal skill, but it was
  measured on training years, and it is not the test result.
- Skill varies from year to year (0.59 to 0.74). The weakest steps are the
  first two, which have only 7 and 8 training years.


8. CHALLENGES AND FIXES
-----------------------
1. Compute on a student laptop. Each setting took 15 to 68 seconds (five
   folds). The script uses half the cores, saves after every setting and can
   resume, so the computer stays usable and nothing is lost if a run is
   stopped.
2. Choice of tuning score. Tuning on spatial CV does not guarantee better
   temporal skill. It was kept because T7-24 named spatial CV for the
   comparison. Tuning on forward chaining instead was not tried.
3. Number of runs. Three tuned runs triple the compute. 12 settings per run
   kept the total manageable.


9. LIMITATIONS
--------------
- The best depth (14) and the best max_features (0.5) are the largest values
  in the search. Larger values were not tried, and a small gain is possible.
  The fold-to-fold SD of the AUC is about 0.014, so a gain under 0.01 would
  not be visible.
- Only 12 of 54 settings were tried in each run.
- The spatial CV score of the winner is slightly optimistic, because the
  setting was chosen on the same folds that score it.
- The difference between 6a and 6c (0.0015) is far smaller than the fold SD,
  so the two are not distinguishable. The choice of 6a rests on the declared
  rule, not on a real difference.
- Forward chaining is not blocked in space. It matches the real test, which
  scores the same area, but it does not measure performance on unseen areas.
- The first forward chaining steps train on only 7 or 8 years.
- AUC measures ranking only. The model output is a relative susceptibility
  score and not a calibrated probability of fire (D21).
- The 2016 to 2024 years were scored in Trial 6, so they are not a blind test.
- Test years are about 10% wetter than the training normal (Phase 4), which
  may reduce the test score.

  whether this affected the model, three Random Forests were tuned and
  validated separately, with both predictors, with burn count only and with
  years since burn only. The rule for choosing between them was fixed before
  the runs. The model with both predictors was selected (spatial CV AUC 0.759,
