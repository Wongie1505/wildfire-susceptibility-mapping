PHASE 2: FIRE EVENTS
====================

Status: COMPLETE. The first run included 2001 and 2025, which are outside the
study window. The script now keeps 2002 to 2024 only. Re-run once to confirm
the corrected totals in section 5.


1. PURPOSE
----------
Turn the raw MODIS fire detections into fire events, one per event, with
every reduction counted. The events are the labels for the model.


2. WHAT WAS DONE
----------------
1. Read the raw archive and build a timestamp from the date and time.
2. Keep July to December.
3. Keep 2002 to 2024 (added after the first run).
4. Keep confidence 75 and above.
5. Keep detections inside the Chikangawa boundary (EPSG:32736).
6. Group detections within 1000 m and 4 days of each other into one event.
   Grouping is chained: if A is close to B and B is close to C, all three
   form one event.
7. Keep one detection per event: highest confidence, earliest time on ties.
8. Write a per-year table with a train or test label.

Folder: Phase_2_fire_events/
Script: phase2_fire_events.py

6. WHY IT MATTERS
-----------------
- The model trains on 2002 to 2015 and tests on 2016 to 2024. Fires from
  2001 and 2025 belong to neither period, so they must not enter the labels
  unless they are kept deliberately as a separate check.
- The events, not the raw detections, are the labels. One large fire
  recorded as 40 detections should count once.
- The test period has about 198 events a year (1,783 over 9 years), and the
  training period about 176 (2,461 over 14 years). The rate does not jump
  sharply at the 2015 to 2016 boundary, so the test years are not a
  different regime from the training years.


7. WHAT THE DISSERTATION SHOULD NOTE
------------------------------------
- Event rule: 1000 m and 4 days, chained, using timestamps. An earlier run
  of the same rule, which grouped by whole days, gave 4,375 events. Use the
  timestamp version in the text and say so.
- Confidence threshold: 75 and above, on the MODIS confidence value.
- Boundary test: detection points inside the boundary polygon. Points on
  the edge are not counted.
- Period: 2002 to 2015 for training, 2016 to 2024 for testing. The 2001 and
  2025 records are excluded and are not used anywhere in the model.
