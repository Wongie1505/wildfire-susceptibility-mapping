1. PURPOSE
Build an independent burn reference from Sentinel-2 (a different sensor from MODIS) for the 2016 to 2024 test years, on the 500 m grid used by the modelling table.

2. METHOD (scripts/s2_burn_export.js)
- Source: COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED, cloud-masked with QA60 (bits 10 and 11).
- Primary windows: pre = 1 to 30 June of Y; post = 1 January to 31 March of Y+1.
- Fallback windows, used only where the primary window is short: pre = 1 May to 30 June; post = 1 January to 30 April of Y+1. Thresholds: MIN_PRE = 5 scenes, MIN_POST = 10 scenes. Fixed from the scene counts before the re-run, not from burned area or agreement.
- NBR = (B8 - B12) / (B8 + B12); dNBR = NBR(pre) - NBR(post).
- Burned pixel: dNBR > 0.27 and NBR(pre) > 0.10. Primary cut-off fixed before scoring.
- Exports per season: burned_fraction and valid_fraction on the 500 m grid, EPSG:32736, origin at multiples of 500 m.
- Output: nine GeoTIFFs in data/ (S2_burn_2016 to S2_burn_2024).
